Proof of Value (POV) Validation & Pipeline Reusability

Prior to this phase, a Proof of Value (POV) to validate core architectural assumptions was executed. We need to analyse and understand the foundational data pipelines engineered during the POV if they are fully reusable. Because the initial ingestion and transformation logic was built adhering to production-grade Databricks Medallion standards (PySpark & Delta Lake), we will directly port and scale these assets. This strategic advantage eliminates redundant development, accelerates our time-to-market for Phase 1, and significantly de-risks the delivery timeline.

Daily Finance Operating View Scope (7 Focus Areas)

Functional coverage required before start of business each working day.

  • 1. Cash Position & Liquidity

    Current balance, 7-day forecast (AR/AP timing), inflows/outflows, funding risks.

  • 2. Receivables & Collections

    Cash collected yesterday, past-due accounts, commitments, billing disputes, daily priorities.

  • 3. Accounts Payable

    Critical vendor payments due, credit hold risk, approval bottlenecks, major disbursements.

  • 4. Billing & Revenue Capture

    Invoices issued yesterday, unbilled work, open change orders, unbilled revenue at risk.

  • 5. Controls & Close

    Approval delays, reconciliation exceptions, items needing action, month-end close progress.

  • 6. Payroll & Compliance

    Payroll readiness, missing timesheets, expense reports, tax & compliance alerts.

  • 7. Plan of Day & Actions

    Top collection target, top payment priority, critical billing objective, escalations with named owners.

Data Engineering Enhancements & Risk Mitigations

Proactive architectural recommendations to strengthen Phase 1 execution.

Proposed · Pending Client Review

  • 1. Adopt Delta Live Tables (DLT) for Ingestion & DQ

    Instead of raw PySpark scripts, utilize Databricks DLT for Bronze/Silver. DLT offers built-in expectations for DQ thresholds, auto-lineage, and automatic pipeline retries with zero manual orchestration setup.

  • 2. Synthetic Snapshot Backfill Strategy

    Since daily snapshots cannot be backfilled from source systems, create a synthetic backfill script using historical log tables during Week 2 to give executives 90-day trendlines immediately on Day 1.

  • 3. FinOps & Serverless Compute Governance

    Enforce Databricks Serverless SQL for Genie & Dashboards, and set auto-termination on ETL clusters to 15 minutes. Implement budget alert webhooks to prevent compute cost surprises.

  • 4. Contractual SLA on Client Source Remediation

    Establish a strict 48-hour client SLA for fixing source-side data errors. Add an automated fallback layer to quarantine bad records so remaining clean data can still populate morning dashboards.
